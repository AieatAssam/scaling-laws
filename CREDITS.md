# Credits

## Music

All music is instrumental. Each track is converted to OGG Vorbis (stereo, 44.1 kHz) and loudness-normalised to about -18 LUFS. Tracks are listed in game order (stage 0 to stage 4). The machine-readable manifest is [assets/audio/music/tracks.json](assets/audio/music/tracks.json).

1. **Flowerbed Fields [Loop]** by Zane Little Music. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/flowerbed-fields-loop. Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. Changes: converted WAV to OGG Vorbis, loudness normalised. Not trimmed or faded. File: `00_garage.ogg`.

2. **Beyond cosmos** by Centurion_of_war. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/beyond-cosmos. Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. Changes: converted to OGG Vorbis, loudness normalised. Not trimmed or faded. File: `01_startup.ogg`.

3. **Cybermatic pulse (LOOP)** by Alexandr Zhelanov. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/cybermatic-pulse. Licence: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Changes: seamless loop kept whole (3:54, not trimmed, no fade), loudness normalised, converted to OGG. Author's requested attribution links: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCxmng6_DMIayDwkiWGVzVRQ and https://soundcloud.com/alexandr-zhelanov. File: `02_datacentre.ogg`.

4. **Drifting Through the Nebula (Ambient)** by HitCtrl. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/drifting-through-the-nebula-ambient. Licence: CC BY 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/. Changes: trimmed to 3:00 with a 3 s fade-out, loudness normalised, converted to OGG. File: `03_planetary.ogg`.

5. **Biolumina** by Tsorthan Grove. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/biolumina. Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. Changes: trimmed to 3:00 with a 3 s fade-out, loudness normalised, converted to OGG. File: `04_beyond.ogg`.

Sound effects are original, generated by `tools/audio/gen_sfx.py`, and released with the game.
