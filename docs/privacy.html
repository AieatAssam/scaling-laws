Privacy policy for Scaling Laws

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Scaling Laws is a single-player idle game. This policy explains what it stores and what it does not do.

Your data stays on your device

  • Your progress is saved locally. In the Android app it is saved in the app's private storage. In the web version it is saved in your browser's storage for this site.
  • The save file is never sent anywhere. The developer cannot see it, and nobody else can either.

What the game does not do

  • It makes no network requests. It does not contact a server, and it does not load analytics, advertising or tracking code.
  • It does not collect personal data, device identifiers, location, contacts or anything else about you.
  • It does not show ads and does not share data with third parties.
  • It has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It has no in-app purchases and no payment or billing code.
  • It works offline.

Permissions

The Android app asks for no permissions. It does not request network access.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address and the date above will be updated.

Contact

If you have a question about this policy, open an issue at https://github.com/AieatAssam/scaling-laws/issues
